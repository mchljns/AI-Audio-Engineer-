Mix Assistant for Mac

Auto-mixing for the Behringer XR18. It runs on the booth Mac, and you use it from an iPad.

Download Mix Assistant

For Macs on macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon or Intel). Free. About 2 minutes to install. Needs the internet while installing.

Install

  1. Open the download. In your Downloads folder, double-click Mix Assistant Installer.

  2. The first time only, macOS asks you to confirm. It says it can't verify the app. Click Done, then:

    • Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
    • Scroll down to "Mix Assistant Installer" was blocked and click Open Anyway.
    • Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click Open.

    This happens because the installer isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate.

  3. Click Install. A message shows while it works. When it's done, Mix Assistant opens in your browser.

After that

  • Mix Assistant starts by itself whenever someone logs in to the Mac. macOS may show a Background Items Added notice: that's it.
  • Open it any time with the Mix Assistant app on the Desktop.
  • Updates, practice mode and troubleshooting reports are buttons in the app's This Mac card. You never need Terminal.
  • Connect each iPad once, with nothing to type: put it on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac, click Connect an iPad in Mix Assistant on the Mac, point the iPad's Camera at the QR code and tap the link, then Share → Add to Home Screen. After that, just tap the icon.

Running the installer again updates Mix Assistant and keeps all its settings. You can also update from the This Mac card.
