                      ProPresenter

                      When the ProPresenter operator puts up a song, Mix Assistant moves to that song here, so its song mix comes in by itself. It only reads which song is up; it never controls ProPresenter.

                      1. On the ProPresenter computer: ProPresenter → Settings → Network, turn on Enable Network. It shows an IP address and a port.
                      2. Type them here once and tap Save. Needs ProPresenter 7.9 or later.
                      3. Both computers must be on the same network (see the note below).

                      Song names are matched loosely: "Holy Forever (Live) - Chris Tomlin" in ProPresenter finds "Holy Forever" in the set. Announcements, sermon slides and anything that isn't a song in today's set are ignored, and you can still tap songs yourself.

                      Network: this Mac reaches ProPresenter over its Wi-Fi. If both are on the school's open Wi-Fi it usually works, but some guest networks block computers from talking to each other. If it says it can't reach ProPresenter, join the ProPresenter computer to the same network as this Mac's Wi-Fi.

                      Reverb

                      Delay time

                      Mix Assistant hears the tempo live (from the click when tracks are running, otherwise the tracks or a steady strum) and works out the delay time for each song marked "light delay". Steady tempos are remembered for that song.

                      Good starting settings (set once in X Air)

                      Reverb (FX1): Plate or Vintage Room/Hall · Decay 1.4–1.8 s · Pre-delay 25–35 ms · Low cut 200–250 Hz · High cut / damping 6–7 kHz · Return fader around −5 dB. Singers: send "Normal" in their Tone.
                      Delay (FX2): Stereo Delay · Time from the song's tempo (shown on each song at soundcheck; a dotted eighth is the usual worship delay) · Feedback 15–25% (2–3 repeats) · Low cut 300 Hz · High cut 5 kHz · Mix 100% (wet). Mix Assistant only feeds it the lead singer on songs marked "light delay".

                      Song mix amounts

                      Set up the PA

                      About 15 minutes, before the service. Do the steps in order. All the mixing happens on the XR18; the PRX ONE and Passport are set once, written down, and left alone. Signal path: XR18 main L + R → PRX ONE (stage right, with its sub) → pass-thru → Passport (the fill on the other side).

                      1 Prepare the speakers

                      2 Set each channel's gain on the XR18

                      Ask each person to talk or sing at their normal loudest, one at a time. Adjust that channel's gain in the X Air app until it says Good here (peaks around -18 to -12). For wireless mics, turn the receiver's output up first, then use less XR18 gain: that keeps hiss down.

                        3 Set the PRX ONE

                        1. On the XR18, set the main fader to 0 dB.
                        2. Play steady sound through the PA: the test noise below, or have the band play at service level.
                        3. Raise the PRX ONE input gain until its signal light shows strong signal but never clip (red).
                        4. Stand in the middle of the room with a phone sound-meter app. Raise the PRX ONE master until speaking is about 75–80 dBA and the loudest worship about 85–90 dBA.
                        5. Turn the PRX ONE master down and check the Passport gets quieter too. If it does, set the PRX ONE first and don't touch it afterwards.
                        Test noise

                        A steady "shhh" played from the booth Mac through the music channel. Turn the speakers down before starting it.

                        4 Set the Passport fill

                        1. Keep the test noise (or a song) playing.
                        2. Walk slowly from the PRX ONE side of the room to the Passport side.
                        3. Set the Passport's channel level so the sound stays about as loud the whole way across, and voices still seem to come from the stage, not the Passport.
                        4. Leave the Passport's master and EQ alone after that.

                        5 Check for hiss

                        Everyone quiet, test noise off, wait 10 seconds. Channels with noticeable hiss are listed. Mute anything unused (Pre-service check → Mute unused channels). For a hissy wireless mic: turn its receiver's output up and its XR18 gain down, and go easy on compressor makeup gain.

                          Hiss still there with the XR18 main fader all the way down? Then it's the speakers: lower the PRX ONE input gain and raise the XR18 main fader instead.
